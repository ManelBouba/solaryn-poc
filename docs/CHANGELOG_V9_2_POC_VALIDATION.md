# V9.2 POC Validation Change Log

V9.2 separates two stages that had been conflated in V9/V9.1:

1. **PoC validation:** demonstrate that real module offers respond differently to hourly climate/operating conditions and produce a reproducible ranking and economic switching threshold.
2. **Bankability-grade decision:** require stronger module-specific evidence and uncertainty characterization.

In V9.2, stage 2 remains visible as a diagnostic but no longer suppresses stage 1.
